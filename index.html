No. We start with your current experience. An initial assessment helps shape an approach suited to where you are and what you want to achieve.

The plan considers your routine and available equipment. Delivery, coaching channels, and the frequency of adjustments are agreed with the trainer before you sign up.

Your training frequency is tailored to your availability, experience, and goals. The first conversation helps identify a routine you can maintain.
